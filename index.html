01 / About Me

Technical mindset. Practical experience.

I'm George Oommen, a Computer Systems Technician graduate from Seneca Polytechnic with High Honours and a 4.0/4.0 CGPA. My experience combines real-world IT support with hands-on work in endpoint management, networking, cloud infrastructure and security.

My approach to IT is based on structured troubleshooting. When something breaks, I want to understand the environment, isolate the problem, identify the root cause, test the solution and document what changed.

I've applied that approach in professional service-desk and technology environments through Windows troubleshooting, Microsoft 365, endpoint deployment, hardware support, asset management, remote support, Microsoft Intune and infrastructure projects.

Outside production environments, I use virtual machines and cloud labs to go deeper into networking, systems administration and security. These projects give me a place to experiment safely, investigate failures and turn what I learn into documented technical knowledge.
